Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when using Spendly:

• Account Information: Your name, email address, and hashed password when you create an account.
• Financial Data: Expense entries, transaction amounts, categories, descriptions, and dates that you log into the application.
• Usage Data: Basic technical logs such as browser type and timestamps to help improve stability and security.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect strictly to deliver and improve our services:

• To provide you with expense tracking, budgeting statistics, and category analytics.
• To maintain your account security and verify your identity upon login.
• To provide customer support and respond to inquiries.

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal financial records or transaction logs to advertisers or third-party data brokers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in encrypted databases. We implement standard industry security practices, including password hashing and secure HTTPS protocols, to safeguard your sensitive information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not share your financial entries with external third parties. We may utilize trusted third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting and database services) solely to operate the platform under strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, please contact us at privacy@spendly.com.
